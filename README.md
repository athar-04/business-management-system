# Business Management System (BMS)

A full-stack Business Management System designed to help businesses digitize and manage their day-to-day operations from a centralized application.

The system is being developed as a scalable platform for managing products, inventory, godowns, purchases, sales, customers, suppliers, payments, expenses, and intelligent business insights.

The frontend and backend are deployed as separate services and communicate through REST APIs.

---

#  Authentication Flow

1. A user registers through the frontend.
2. The registration request is sent to the Spring Boot backend.
3. The backend validates the request.
4. The password is securely encoded.
5. User and business-related data are stored in PostgreSQL.
6. The user logs in using email and password.
7. Spring Security authenticates the credentials.
8. The backend generates a JWT token.
9. The frontend stores the token.
10. Axios attaches the token as a `Bearer` token to protected API requests.
11. The backend validates the JWT before granting access.

## Phase 2 — AI & ML Powered Business Analytics 

**Status: Planned**

Phase 2 focuses on using the data generated by the business management system to provide intelligent analysis, predictions, insights, and recommendations.

The goal is to move beyond simple record management and help the business owner make better decisions using **Machine Learning and AI**.

### Planned Capabilities

####  Intelligent Business Analytics

- Analyze sales data
- Analyze purchase patterns
- Analyze expenses
- Identify business trends
- Generate meaningful business insights

####  Dead Stock Analysis

- Identify products with very low movement
- Detect potential dead stock
- Analyze product movement history
- Highlight inventory that may be blocking capital

####  Sales and Demand Forecasting

- Analyze historical sales patterns
- Predict future demand
- Identify seasonal trends
- Support better purchase planning

####  Smart Inventory Insights

- Identify low-stock patterns
- Suggest possible reorder requirements
- Analyze inventory movement
- Provide inventory-related recommendations

####  AI-Generated Insights

- Generate summaries from business data
- Explain important trends in natural language
- Highlight unusual changes in sales or inventory
- Provide decision-support recommendations
